## Hardware layout
- Storage zone: 40 × 20 sites.
- Entanglement zone: 2 × 20 sites, placed to the right of the storage zone.
- Storage-zone atom indices are assigned column by column, vertically within each column, starting from the column closest to the entanglement zone and continuing toward the farthest column.
- 2q gate happen in entanglement zone

## AOD constraints
The 2D AOD tweezer array is generated by the Cartesian product of X-axis and Y-axis RF tones:

- Each X tone defines one movable column.
- Each Y tone defines one movable row.
- A tweezer may appear at every intersection of an active X tone and an active Y tone.
- Therefore, X and Y coordinates cannot be treated as independently controllable for every atom.

Movement rules:

1. Changing an X tone moves the entire corresponding column in the X direction.
   Every atom using that X tone must move together with the same X trajectory.

2. Changing a Y tone moves the entire corresponding row in the Y direction.
   Every atom using that Y tone must move together with the same Y trajectory.

3. Two atoms may move in parallel only when their required trajectories are compatible:
   - atoms sharing the same X tone must have identical X motion;
   - atoms sharing the same Y tone must have identical Y motion;
   - atoms requiring different motions must use different tones.
