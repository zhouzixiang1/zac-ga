\title{GA-LK: Joint Placement with Multilayer Look-Ahead\\
for Zoned Neutral-Atom Architectures}

\author{
\IEEEauthorblockN{Zixiang Zhou}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Hangzhou Dianzi University\\
1158 No. 2 Street, Baiyang Subdistrict, Qiantang District\\
Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China\\
zhouzixiang@hdu.edu.cn}
}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Zoned neutral-atom quantum processors isolate stored atoms to reduce idle-atom excitation, while inter-layer placement and rearrangement affect trap-transfer losses and execution time. Two representative reuse-aware and routing-aware placement methods use adjacent layers and the next interaction partner to guide placement. These methods do not jointly search over residency between nonadjacent layers, two-qubit gate sites, and return storage sites. Neither estimates the ensuing rearrangement and idle-excitation losses from each candidate across multiple future gate layers. We present GA-LK, a joint placement method with multilayer look-ahead. Genetic search coordinates gate-site and residency choices, while matching assigns return storage sites, jointly optimizing the three decisions. Each candidate is first checked against occupancy and acousto-optic deflector constraints. Evaluation then uses atom positions, trap occupancy, and clocks after current-layer execution to estimate transport, idle-excitation, and coherence losses over subsequent layers. Weights decay with layer distance, allowing current placement to account for execution costs across multiple future layers. Evaluation uses two benchmark sets containing 18 circuits with 11--98 qubits and 154 input circuits with 3--16 qubits, respectively. Among circuits with valid results from all methods, benchmark-level comparisons show up to \PaperPercentGain{\QMAPMFourF}{\QMAPMOneF} higher geometric-mean model fidelity than existing compilers. The mean rearrangement batch count decreases by up to \PaperPercentReduction{\QMAPMFourB}{\QMAPMOneB}.
\end{abstract}
